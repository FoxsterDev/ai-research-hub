## Qualification and rollout gates

1. Installed hookless CLIs cannot prove exact post-truncation outbound delivery,
   immutable backend identity, exclusive brokered writes or separated transport.
   Their task oracles can pass while numeric fitness remains `null`. A different
   supported surface must supply those capabilities before adoption qualification.
2. A qualified blinded F6 exposure is required to remove the holdout cap. Authoring
   controls and signatures alone do not provide it; an exhausted fixture identity
   must be rotated through newly authored content.
3. Statistical qualification requires the full preregistered sample size,
   dependence contract and corrected uncertainty bounds. Two or three smoke
   repeats do not establish autonomous fitness.
4. A shared-core experiment candidate needs eligible control/treatment evidence
   plus every required supported-profile and host-consumer regression entry.
   Statistical acceptance cannot authorize live application.
5. Advisory requires live conformance. Blocking follows at least fourteen days
   of reviewed advisory telemetry and a satisfied false-block budget. The default
   remains disabled, with an explicit off switch.
6. Remote publication, merging and production application are separate actions.
   The three-platform CI matrix must actually execute on the remote before any
   CI-green publication claim. Local commits preserve the tested state.
